# Workstream f-accounts: accounts, alerts, email and Pro

Goal: one short step from "I care about this ground" to a confirmed alert, alerts that are worth opening, and a Pro path where limits are felt.

Read `common.md`, `contracts.md` and `build-specs.md` in this folder first. Your files: `resources/views/pages/auth/*`, `resources/views/layouts/auth/*`, `resources/views/pages/watch-areas.blade.php`, a new `/watch` page and `<livewire:watch-this-area />`, `app/Models/WatchArea.php`, `app/Models/User.php`, new migrations, `app/Alerts/*`, `app/Mail/*` (alerts), `resources/views/mail/watch-area-digest.blade.php`, `resources/views/pages/pro.blade.php`, `resources/views/pages/settings/*`, Fortify config and actions, your route block in `routes/web.php`, tests. Add one line `<livewire:watch-this-area />` at the top of the panel in `resources/views/pages/map.blade.php`, in its own block (e-activity owns the rest of that file).

## Items

- **W1 Email first, magic link (P0, L).** Build spec 5 with Gemini's notes. `watch_areas` gains `kind` (`circle`, `tenement`, `holder`), nullable centre and radius, `tenid`, `holder_slug`, `confirmed_at`, `include_covered_deaths`; `area` is the circle buffer or the tenement outline plus 2 km; holder watches match `tenement_events.holders` by `pw_slug`. `users.password` nullable. The panel listens for `watch-shape`, shows live counts for the shape (debounced server call), the two free alert types ticked and the Pro types locked, an email field, "Email me when this changes", and "One email after a night with changes. Unsubscribe from any email." Submit: same validation, honeypot and rate limits as the digest sign-up; create the user (email only) and an unconfirmed area; send a signed confirm link valid 7 days. Confirm GET shows a button; POST verifies the email, confirms, signs in with remember-me, and lands on `/watch-areas` with "Watching <name>. Your first email comes after the next extract with changes." Existing users: same flow, the area joins their account (free limit applies). Login offers "Email me a login link" (signed, GET shows a button, POST signs in). Settings: setting a password is optional; fix the security and delete-account pages for users without a password. `AlertMatcher` skips unconfirmed areas. `/watch?tenement=<slug>` and `?holder=<slug>` start the flow with that shape.
- **W2 Branded auth (P0).** Use the split auth layout: Pegwatch mark and pitch on one side ("Know when ground near you changes"), a real alert preview instead of the starter kit's quotes; no starter-kit wording; remove the hard-coded `dark` class from the auth layout (V6).
- **G15 Alerts skip covered deaths (P0).** Default: no alert for conversions, amalgamations, or deaths that leave under 1 ha uncovered (`tenement_events.details`: `kind`, `area_ha`, `uncovered_share`); a per-area opt-in "Also tell me about deaths that leave no ground uncovered".
- **E1 Alert email (P0).** Subject with area and counts ("Kalgoorlie east: 1 tenement dead, 2 releases scheduled"); RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (copy from the digest mail) and a signed one-click "Stop alerts for this area" link that needs no login; items grouped by urgency (releases, deaths, other); sentence-case types; uncovered hectares for deaths; "Open the map" linking to the area's view (`/#map=…`); TENGRAPH as a link. If `og.area` exists (g-visual), include the area image (guard with `Route::has`).
- **E3 (P1).** Attach an .ics (`Ics::release`) to emails with a release scheduled. Day-before and hour-before reminders need a scheduler (`routes/console.php` has none): add a scheduled command and say in your report that Laravel Cloud needs the scheduler enabled.
- **W3, O15 Watch page (P1, P2).** Edit radius after saving with presets (5, 10, 25, 50 km), a centre pin, place search when `places` exists, bounds fitted to the radius, the tenements inside, "In the last 30 days: <n> deaths, <n> applications", an email preview, tenement popups while picking, and the last 20 alert emails sent with the next check time.
- **W4, G10, D2 Pro paths (P1).** Locked Pro alert types and a locked "Add a second area" card; on `/pro`: show the pre-sale goal the page already loads, scroll phones to the email field when a plan is tapped, check marks instead of "Yes", a short FAQ (refund, start date), lead with a real alert example and a GPX-on-phone example, and one name for the page ("Pricing" in the nav means the title should match; pick "Plans"). Locked "Export GPX / KML" buttons on tenement and watch pages link to `/pro?ref=export` (on the tenement page, add it only if c-tenement has not; say so).
- **B18 (P1).** Guests redirected to log in see why ("Log in to see your watch area").
- **T7, H7 backends (P1).** Tenement and holder watch kinds above; free plan: one area and one holder watch.
